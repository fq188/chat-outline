Why does my sourdough come out dense in the middle even after a 12-hour cold proof?
09:41
A tight crumb after a long cold proof usually means the dough was under-fermented before shaping. Judge by rise, not by the clock: it should grow 50–70% and feel airy and jiggly before it goes into the fridge.

My kitchen sits around 26°C in summer. How much starter should I use?
At 26°C, drop the starter to about 15% of the flour weight and shorten the bulk ferment. Warm kitchens ferment much faster, so start checking for the jiggle at the 4-hour mark instead of 6.
Can I keep the Dutch oven at 250°C for the whole bake?
Better to drop the heat after the lid comes off. Bake 20 minutes covered at 250°C to build the spring, then uncover and finish 20–25 minutes at 220°C so the crust sets without scorching the base.
